Why it matters

French is the edge in a crowded pool

English Express Entry pools keep getting more competitive, and French has become one of the strongest advantages an applicant can add. Here is what each level does for you.

NCLC 5

Your first milestone

French counts as your second official language, and you start earning CRS points for each ability from NCLC 5. Some routes, such as the Francophone Community Immigration Pilot, ask for NCLC 5 in French.

NCLC 7

The big unlock

NCLC 7 in all four abilities opens the French-language category draws and up to 50 additional CRS points (with at least CLB 5 in English). Recent French draws have had lower cut-offs than general rounds.

TCF

One exam, many doors

TCF Canada is accepted by IRCC for Express Entry and other federal programs. Every lesson here is built around how TCF Canada is scored.

This program works toward CLB 5 as a first milestone, and motivated students keep going toward NCLC 7. Immigration rules change often; always check the current requirements on canada.ca for your own pathway.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need any prior French knowledge?

None at all. The program starts on Day 1 with the French alphabet. If you already have A2 or B1 French, you will move through Phase 1 faster, which leaves more time for the skills that decide your score.

Are there session recordings I can watch later?

No, and that's deliberate. The live session is where the learning happens: the questions you ask and the mistakes you correct in the moment are what move your score.

What if I don't understand something in one class?

We do more classes. The program isn't bound to a fixed number of sessions. If you need one more class, you get one more. If you need five, you get five.

How are classes delivered?

Live, one-on-one online sessions on Zoom or Google Meet. There are no pre-recorded videos and no self-paced modules.

Is this for TCF Canada specifically, not TEF or DELF?

Yes. Every lesson is built around the format and scoring of TCF Canada, which IRCC accepts for immigration. Its format differs from TEF, DELF and DALF.

I already have a strong English CRS score. Do I still need French?

It can make a real difference. French earns second-language points from NCLC 5 in each ability, and NCLC 7 in all four opens the French-language draws and up to 50 additional CRS points if your English is at least CLB 5. CLB 5 is the first milestone on that path.

What if I want CLB 6, CLB 7 or higher?

CLB 5 is the first target. The B1 curriculum in Phase 3 naturally pushes motivated students further. Your starting level, daily consistency and the extra sessions you take all affect how far you go.
